Walk through the sample proof

The theorem assumes h : policy e s = .permit and must produce a proof of permitRequirements e s. by starts a tactic script that constructs that proof.

  1. unfold policy at h exposes the function's chain of conditionals inside the hypothesis.
  2. split at h considers both branches of the next conditional.
  3. <;> try contradiction tries to close each resulting goal. A branch returning defer, escalate or protectiveShutdown conflicts with h, which requires permit.
  4. After six splits, only the final ready test remains. next hr names its successful branch's evidence, ready e s = true.
  5. exact ready_sound e s hr supplies precisely the proof of the requirements that we need. The final contradiction rules out deny = permit.

A conjunction is a pair of proofs. .1 selects its first part; .2 selects its second. The requirements are nested as completed ∧ (regime ∧ (credential ∧ (expiry ∧ (transport ∧ permission)))). Thus .2.2.1 retrieves the credential proof and .2.2.2.1 retrieves the expiry proof.

The final ⟨adapterProof, policyProof⟩ constructs another pair: successful adaptation satisfies the adapter contract, and a permitted decision satisfies the policy contract. These are conditional proofs about the supplied observations, not proofs that those observations describe reality.
