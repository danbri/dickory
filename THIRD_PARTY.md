# Third-party material

The two files in `labs/rdf11/specifications/` are unmodified copies of the W3C Recommendations dated 25 February 2014:

- [RDF 1.1 Semantics](https://www.w3.org/TR/2014/REC-rdf11-mt-20140225/)
- [RDF 1.1 Concepts and Abstract Syntax](https://www.w3.org/TR/2014/REC-rdf11-concepts-20140225/)

Their original copyright and W3C document-licence notices remain in the files. They are not relicensed under this repository's Apache 2.0 licence. The review viewer embeds these archived sources and reproduces portions alongside annotations; the annotations are this project's work, not W3C endorsements or accepted corrections.

The catalogue records SHA-256 identities and Unicode scalar ranges. `.gitattributes` disables line-ending conversion for the archived documents. CI rechecks their exact bytes before producing the site.

Lean and the GitHub Actions used by CI are separately distributed dependencies. The W3C RDF tests repository is referenced at a pinned revision; it is not included here and has not yet been connected to this fresh formal model.
